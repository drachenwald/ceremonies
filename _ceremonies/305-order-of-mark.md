# Notes

- _The candidate for and recipient of the elevation is referred to as "candidate" throughout the ceremony for the purpose of clarity. Where "[Candidate]" is noted, replace with the candidate's name with or without title as appropriate._

- _The invocation of vigil is part of a different court than the elevation, typically held on the previous day. If multiple vigils are held concurrently, the invocations can be combined. If there is no vigil to be held, the invocation does not happen either._

- _Both instances of summons and the ending of the elevation ceremony can be altered freely to suit the occasion. Other parts as well as their order can be altered as well, but all parts typically exist in some form._

- _The speakers giving testimony should be advised to keep their speeches brief and to the point. If the sponsor is arranging the speeches beforehand, this should be done by the sponsor. If the speakers are being arranged on site, it is recommended that the court herald contacts the speakers and reminds them of both the expected content and the length of their speeches._

- _An archer-specific variant of the oath of fealty is included. It requires preparations so must be agreed upon beforehand. Otherwise the standard fealty (also included) can be used._

# Ceremonies

## Invocation of Vigil

### 1 Summons

Royal Herald
: Their Majesties call before them _[Candidate]_.

_(The candidate approaches.)_

Crown
: _(The monarchs address the candidate as they see fit.)_

Herald, please proceed.

Royal Herald
: Their Majesties command the presence of the Order of the Mark.

_(They come, possibly led by their herald, noted as Mark Herald from now on.)_

Crown
: Our good peers, we have asked you here to lend us your aid and counsel in a manner agreed with you before. Welcome.

### 2 Invocation

_The main Invocation text can be read by the herald in full, or in part delegated to companions of each peerage order._

Crown
: _[Candidate]_, your skill in the arts of ranged warfare is known to us, your virtues beyond reproach. It is our wish that you will accept from us the title of Master of the Mark. Will you consider this honour?

Royal Herald
: It is necessary that I address you concerning aspects of peerage and chivalric virtues. It is often said that peerage is an honour. It is surely that, but more, it is a responsibility which you should not take upon yourself lightly but with sobriety and sincerity. The oath you will be offered will bind you in fealty to the Crown, and it is well that you should understand what you are about to promise.

Royal Herald or a companion of Chivalry
: The knights and the masters of arms are the Order of Chivalry, but no less chivalrous behaviour will be expected of you. As a peer of the realm you must endeavour to live, as you have until now, by the rules of courtesy and gentle behaviour. Deal justly and fairly with all, regardless of their station, and be an example others can follow.

Royal Herald or a companion of Defence
: The Order of Defence is the order noted for its deadly art of weapons, but you too are expected to apply to your own art and scholarship utmost tenacity and grace, to defend what is right, and to share your knowledge with those willing to learn.

Royal Herald or a companion of the Pelican
: The Order of the Pelican is the order of service, but each companion of the Mark, too, has the responsibility to serve: not only the Crown, but the land as a whole, performing to the best of your ability. A peer must be willing to do good works, support those in need, and lead wisely.

Royal Herald or a companion of the Laurel
: The Order of the Laurel is the order most noted for artistry and scholarship, but also a companion of the Mark must encourage the arts and sciences both by appreciating them and by participating in their practice. One of the greatest defences of a land is making it whole and harmonious, and, devoid of the arts and sciences, that it cannot be.

Royal Herald or Mark Herald
: The Order of the Mark is noted for its precise and accurate art and scholarship of ranged warfare. The master marksmen and artillerists of the order stand as the foremost examples of esperance, not only in ranged warfare, but in all manner of courtliness. A companion of the Mark must display equal grace in victory and in defeat; they must not suffer injustice, but neither should they seek vengeance; they must remember that as their reputation depends on their skill in judging the distance, and accurate finding of their mark, so it also depends upon the way they carry themselves when the fogs of war or wayward winds send their bolts astray. Be mindful that a peer guides not only by their word, but also by the example they set for others.

As a markmaster you need to continue your scholarship and study of your chosen discipline and all other courtly arts, as being a master is being a scholar who is now responsible for not only one's own improvement but the improvement of others as well. Remember the demands and dictates of courtesy, honour, and chivalry, and being true to the order you are about to join, defend them by your life, limb, and heart, both on the fields of combat and in the halls of our courts. Teach the love of them as well as your art to all those who look upon you for guidance.

Royal Herald
: To accept elevation to the peerage is to accept these responsibilities. I pray you keep these words in your heart, as you prepare for your new status.

Crown
: Our peers, please escort the candidate to their vigil to consider our offer and please give them your guidance and council in this matter.

_(The order takes their leave.)_

## The Elevation

### 1 Summons

Herald
: Their Majesties command the presence of all companions of the Order of the Mark.

_(They come, possibly led by their herald, noted as Mark Herald from now on.)_

Royal Herald
: It has long been recognized that not only in the close combat in tourneys and melees of the sword, spear and axe, but also in the study and practice of the well judged and far reaching warfare are the strength and prowess of the kingdom forged. These are practised, honed and taught by those who have dedicated themselves to archery, siege engineering, and the art of thrown weapons.

Therefore was created the Order of the Mark, bearing a Patent of Arms and ranking equal with the orders of Chivalry, the Laurel, Defence, and the Pelican, to recognise those who, possessing all the other skills, virtues and attributes appropriate to members of the peerage, shall also have distinguished themselves by their outstanding achievement in martial arts from afar.

And the tokens of this order are two white bracers or vanguards, and a symbol depicting a set of nesting scale weights within and conjoined to a mascle fleury at the upper point.

Crown
: Good companions of the Mark, bring the candidate forward.

_(A companion of the Mark (escort / sponsor) brings the candidate forth.)_

Companion of the Mark
: Your Majesties, it is my privilege to present _[Candidate]_ that they may be made a companion of our order.

### Testimonies

- _It is not required to have speakers from all of the listed orders._
- _The speaking order can be modified._
- _Each speaker is called by name except the speaker for the people; this can be modified._

Crown
: Herald, please proceed.

Royal Herald
: A peer must be truly a peer, not only by the rank. Before raising _[Candidate]_ to the Order of the Mark, Their Majesties wish to hear how they are seen by those capable of judging their peer-like qualities.

Royal Herald
: The Order of the Mark is known for its unerring judgement in finding the targets with all manner of missiles both in peace and in war. _[Speaker]_ of this order will give testimony for the candidate.

Pelican Speaker
: _(Gives testimony of the virtues of the candidate, e.g. judgement and tenacity.)_

Crown
: Thank you _[Speaker]_.

Royal Herald
: The Order of the Laurel fosters arts and sciences, without which the land is bare and barren. _[Speaker]_ of this order will give testimony for the candidate.

Laurelk Speaker
: _(Gives testimony of the virtues of the candidate, e.g. pursuit of knowledge and beauty.)_

Crown
: Thank you _[Speaker]_.

Royal Herald
: The Order of Chivalry stands as the primary of strength and chivalry. _[Speaker]_ of this order will give testimony for the candidate.

Chivalry Speaker
: _(Gives testimony of the virtues of the candidate, e.g.chivalry and prowess.)_

Crown
: Thank you _[Speaker]_.

Royal Herald
: The Order of the Pelican is the order of service and stewardship. _[Speaker]_ of this order will give testimony for the candidate.

Pelican Speaker
: _(Gives testimony of the virtues of the candidate, e.g. service and loyalty.)_

Crown
: Thank you _[Speaker]_.

Royal Herald
: The Order of Defence is the order of steel, swordsmanship and the art of arms. _[Speaker]_ of this order will give testimony for the candidate.

Defence Speaker
: _(Gives testimony of the virtues of the candidate, e.g. grace and dedication.)_

Crown
: Thank you _[Speaker]_.

Royal Herald
: The Order of the Rose is dedicated to courtesy and honour. _[Speaker]_ of this order will give testimony for the candidate.

Rose Speaker
: _(Gives testimony of the virtues of the candidate, e.g. courtesy and honour.)_

Crown
: Thank you _[Speaker]_.

Royal Herald
: Drachenwald has many peers, but many more who are not, and their voice is mighty. Is there someone from among the people of the realm to speak for the candidate?

Speaker of the people
: _(Speaks of the virtues the candidate exhibits in interacting within the Society, the leadership the candidate provides, and the example they set for others to follow.)_

### 3 Resolution

Mark Herald or Royal Herald
: Your Majesties, it has been testified before the Crown and this court that _[Candidate]_ is a worthy candidate. Do you wish to question the Order of the Mark?

Crown
: Yes we do.

Royal Herald
: Companions of the Order of the Mark. You have heard the testimonies before this court concerning _[Candidate]_. Do you wish to welcome them to your order to share from your cup and join you in pursuits of the ranged martial arts?

Order of the Mark
: Yes indeed!

Royal Herald
: Do you have any bonds that would prevent you from proceeding?

_(If the candidate has existing fealties, they will be resolved. Otherwise the candidate responds that there are none.)_

Crown or Royal Herald
: _[Candidate]_, right mindful of your achievement and service to our land, and responsive to the wishes of your peers, we are minded to create you a companion of the Order of the Mark. Will you accept from us this honour and this badge of your achievement?

Candidate
: I will.

### 4 Bestowing of Regalia

Royal Herald
: Their Majesties wish to adorn the Markmaster _[or other appropriate title]_ with the vestments of the order. Bring forth the medallion.

_(Medallion and its lineage are presented to the Crown.)_

Crown
: _(Monarch puts the medallion around the candidate's neck, saying:)_

Take from our hands this token of your achievement and our esteem.

Royal Herald
: Bring forth the cape.

_(A cape of the order is placed on the candidate's shoulders.)_

Royal Herald
: Brace the Markmaster for the demands of their new station.

_(Bracers or vanguards are attached to the arms of the candidate.)_

Royal Herald
: As bracers and vambraces protect the warrior, so protects a companion of the Mark the realm and the Crown. Their pure white colour symbolises honour, aspirations, and the virtue of esperance that this order embodies and stands for.

The cloak is the outward sign of _[Candidate]_'s new status as a companion of the Order of the Mark and a peer of the realm.

### 5 Swearing of Fealty

#### Fealty: Variant 1 "Standard"

Royal Herald
: _[Candidate]_, do you wish to swear fealty to the Crown at this time?

Candidate
: I do.

_(Note: If the answer is no, skip to the section 'Letters Patent'.)_

_(The Crown receives the sword of state. The candidate places their hands on the sword, the monarchs place their hands upon the candidate's hands.)_

Royal Herald
: Will you swear on your honour to hold _[Sovereign]_ and _[Consort]_ your rightful monarchs, right until the end of their lawful reign, and support their royal might and justice?

Candidate
: I so swear.

Royal Herald
: Do you wish to give the Crown such counsel which is to the benefit of them and their realm, and support them in upholding the laws and fulfilling their coronation oath?

Candidate
: I do.

Royal Herald
: And will you keep secret such matters as the Crown wants to keep in confidence, and not divulge such secrets that could harm their realm?

Candidate
: I will.

Royal Herald
: And we in turn promise to recognise you as a peer of the realm, and grant you and your household, including all your servants, vassals, and apprentices, all the rights and privileges of old, and to protect you and yours with all our royal might and the law of the realm. 			

Sovereign
: So say we _[Name of the Sovereign, title]_.

Consort
: So say we _[Name of the Consort, title]_.

#### Fealty: Variant 2 "Breaking of Arrows"

- _Requires preparations so must be agreed upon beforehand!_
- _The candidate brings arrows they will break during the vow._
- _The Crown has a different script as well as the candidate._

Royal Herald
: _[Candidate]_, do you wish to swear fealty to the Crown at this time?

Candidate
: I do.

_(The Crown receives the sword of state.)_
_(The candidate receives the arrows from their arrow bearer, or otherwaise takes them forth.)_
- _The sword is placed so that one prong of the hilt faces the candidate._
- _The candidate kisses the sword._
- _The candidate __does not__ place their hands on the sword, as this vow goes differently._

Royal Herald
: Will you hold _[Sovereign] and _[Consort]_ your rightful monarchs, right until the end of their lawful reign, and support their royal might and justice?

Candidate
: I so swear, and should I break that promise let me be like this broken arrow.

_(breaks an arrow)_

Royal Herald
: And we in turn promise to you that we strive to be as good liegelords to you as you certainly deserve, and if we break this promise, let us be like a sceptre lost from our royal hand.

Sovereign
: So swear I, _[Sovereign]_.

Consort
: So swear I, _[Consort]_.

Royal Herald
: Will you place your bow and wisdom at their disposal to defend them from all adversaries and your skills at their disposal in teaching archery to all of their subjects willing and able to learn?

Candidate
: I so swear, and should I break that promise let me be like this broken arrow.

_(breaks an arrow)_

Royal Herald
: And we in turn grant you and your household all the rights and privileges of old, and should we go against our word in this, let us be like an orb omitted from our royal hand.

Monarch
: So swear I, _[Sovereign]_.

Consort
: So swear I, _[Consort]_.

Royal Herald
: Do you wish to give the Crown such counsel which is to the benefit of them and their realm, and support them in upholding the laws and fulfilling their coronation oath? And will you keep secret such matters as the Crown wants to keep in confidence, and not divulge such secrets that could harm their realm?

Candidate
: I so swear, and should I break that promise let me be like this broken arrow.

_(breaks an arrow)_

Royal Herald
: And we in turn promise to protect and guide you and yours with the law of the realm, which we shall guard and keep with the fury of a dragon and love of angels, and and should we err and go against this oath, let us be like a dented and forgotten crown.

Monarch Sovereign
: So swear I, _[Sovereign]_.

Monarch Consort
: So swear I, _[Consort]_.

### 6 Letters Patent

Crown
: Herald, make known our royal will.

Royal Herald
: _(Herald reads the letters patent.)_

### 7 End of the Ceremony

Crown
: Congratulations, _[Candidate]_. We entrust you to our Order of the Mark.

Royal Herald
: _(Appropriate call for vivat.)_

The Order may take their leave.
